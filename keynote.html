Spoken Verbal Scrutiny

The Senior Interviewer Dynamic
“You chose PostgreSQL for order processing. Why not MongoDB or DynamoDB?”

Senior interviewers do not test rote recall.
They challenge your system trade-offs under real-time scrutiny.

Spoken conversation.
Not another text chatbot.
